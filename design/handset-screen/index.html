ESP-CCR wired handset — 41 screens, 320 x 172 px, 2 Hz, three keys

Every frame is a real screen file, inlined unchanged: screens/*.svg is the only source this page reads. The numbers are real bench wire lines, quoted under each frame; the caption's last line is the defect that screen exists to prevent. DESIGN.md holds the rules, tokens.css the tokens.

Why the light toggles are there: colour is reserved for meaning (live, ok, warn, crit, held), so no colour must still leave every screen readable — each meaning also has a word and a shape. Sunlight and low light are the two angles where a thin outline disappears; that is why a critical alarm is a solid filled band with dark text on it and never a tint.

The whole navigation model in one drawing: three keys, one cursor, one held gesture. ENTER held 1.5 s goes home from anywhere, which is what makes "every screen is escapable" true without a back key. The red dashed edge is the ACK override — it exists only while a critical FLAGS bit is set, because Manager::ack() suppresses nothing else.

Regenerate with python3 build_index.py. Screens are verified against the panel's own type metrics: no <text> crosses the 320 px edge, no two strings on one baseline overlap, and every number drawn is a token of the wire line quoted in that screen's <desc>.
